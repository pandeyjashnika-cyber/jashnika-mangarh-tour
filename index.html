About Mangarh Bhakti Dham

Bhakti Dham Mandir Mangarh is a renowned spiritual and devotional destination located in Kunda, Pratapgarh, Uttar Pradesh. This sacred temple is associated with Jagadguru Shri Kripalu Ji Maharaj and attracts visitors from across India seeking spiritual experiences and darshan.

The Mangarh Bhakti Dham temple stands as a beacon of faith and devotion in the Pratapgarh region. Whether you are a pilgrim seeking blessings or a traveller interested in experiencing authentic spiritual culture, a visit to this temple offers a meaningful and enriching experience.

Located conveniently near Pratapgarh, the Bhakti Dham Mandir is easily accessible for one-day tours and short pilgrimages. The serene spiritual atmosphere and beautiful temple architecture make it an ideal destination for religious tourism in Uttar Pradesh.
